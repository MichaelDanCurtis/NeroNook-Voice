# Quiet speech and speaker certainty · Voice 0.22.5

Live Parakeet and Nemotron meeting transcription now use an independent, bounded energy estimate for each audio source. The previous fixed loud-speech trigger remains. An additional low-level path compares DC-removed energy with the lower fifth of the previous 50 fixed 64 ms frames, using a four-times noise margin and bounded minimum/maximum thresholds. This retains soft speech that never crossed the old fixed trigger. The existing 350 ms onset prefix and separate Room/Call clocks remain; explicit commits and discontinuities reset calibration. There are no extra models, audio uploads or ambient recordings.

This is an energy heuristic, not a speech classifier. Changing noise, music, reverberation and continuous low-contrast speech can still defeat it. The recognizer still decides whether admitted audio contains words. It retains at most 50 scalar energies and a partial 1,024-sample frame per source; it does not keep whole-meeting PCM. The recognizer's existing utterance limits remain.

Speaker attribution also preserves word-level uncertainty during regrouping. Previously, averaging an overlap-ambiguous word with an under-covered word could falsely label the group as one speaker. Such groups now stay unassigned. Confident words remain separated; source isolation, manual edits and conservative unaligned fallback remain. This does not improve the speaker model itself or establish real names.

The local comparison uses twelve previously cached clean/quiet paired LibriSpeech passages (six readers, 182 reference words) and six deterministic nonspeech controls. Parakeet word errors fall from 13 to 2; Nemotron from 9 to 4. No individual clip regresses; neither baseline nor candidate emits words on the controls. These are development data, not an independent broad WER evaluation. The synthetic controls cannot establish behavior on all real noise.

On the same three cached AMI meeting windows at original/0.1 gain, simulated reference speaker-time outside admitted ASR input windows drops in five of six cases, and stays zero in the sixth. The far-field TS3003c channel drops from 128.644 to 8.726 seconds at original amplitude. This measures input coverage only, not actual recognition, diarization error or noise rejection. The two TS3003c channels are correlated. More admitted audio can increase model work; this study does not measure power or battery use.

Original baseline failures, attempted runs, predictions, frozen input hashes, gate constants and build/test receipts are retained under `reports/meeting-quiet-speakers-2026-10-28/`. Existing recordings/transcripts/notes are not regenerated automatically. Apple Speech defaults and speech/Talk/dictation behavior outside MLX meeting transcription are unchanged.
